          Help & shortcuts

          Getting started
          • Choose, drop or paste (Ctrl+V) videos and images on the start screen — each choice starts a new project. Start with an empty canvas begins with a color clip.
          • Phones: Gallery is quick, Files keeps the original file names.
          • Big videos can be pre-trimmed first — only the chosen part goes onto the timeline.
          • Projects are saved automatically. Tap the logo to get back to the project list.
          Timeline & editing
          • Phones: the playhead stays in the middle — scroll the timeline to move through the video. Desktop: click or drag in the time ruler (or drag the red line) to set the playhead; scrolling only moves the view.
          • Zoom with the 🔍 buttons, two fingers (pinch) or Ctrl + mouse wheel. Fit (the button between them) shows the whole project — or the selected clip.
          • Exact timing: the clip panel's Start field takes a time like 0:43.79; the buttons below move a clip by ±1 s, ±0.1 s or ±0.01 s (desktop: Alt + ←/→, with Shift for 0.01 s). Clips on the magnetic main track follow their order instead.
          • Tap a clip to select it, then drag it. Unselected clips never move, so scrolling is safe.
          • Several clips: desktop — Ctrl/⌘ + click adds or removes a clip, Shift + click selects a range, drag a frame in an empty part of the timeline, Ctrl + A selects everything. Phones — long-press a clip or tap Select, then tap clips; Done ends it. Delete, copy/paste, cut, mute, volume & fades, detach audio and moving work on all selected clips Several clips can be dragged together; clips of the magnetic main track move as a block. Crop, position and rotation apply to all selected pictures.
          • Media (fold-out list in the settings column) shows every file of the project, also unused ones: tap one to select its clips, ＋ / ⧉ adds it again, 🗑 removes an unused file; on desktop drag it onto the timeline.
          • Trim: drag the blue handles at the start or end of the selected clip. On the magnetic main track the following clips move along; elsewhere a clip stops at its neighbours. Videos and sounds can't grow beyond their file; images and color clips can. The edges snap like dragged clips.
          • Cut splits the selected clip at the playhead (or the main track's clip under it).
          • Paste inserts at the playhead — into the main track if copied from there, otherwise as an overlay.
          • The speaker next to the time mutes everything; Mute in the clip panel mutes one clip.
          • Undo / Redo keep 10 steps (adjustable in Settings).
          Tracks & overlays
          • The main track is at the bottom and magnetic: clips follow each other without gaps. Switch the magnet off in its track settings to place clips freely.
          • Audio tracks lie below the main track — sound only; they can't take pictures and the other way round.
          • Add → As overlay puts a video, image or color clip at the playhead on a track above — half size, centred. Higher tracks cover lower ones.
          • Drag a selected clip up or down to another track. No room there, or dropped above the top track? A new track is created.
          • Left of each track: 👁 hide, 🔊 mute. Tap the track's left edge for opacity, volume, order and delete.
          • + above the track headers adds an empty video or audio track — handy as a place to drag clips to. Tracks added this way stay until you delete them; tracks that were created automatically disappear when they become empty.
          • Settings → how many tracks are visible before the timeline scrolls.
          Transitions & fades
          • Where two clips meet, a small + sits on the cut. Tap it to add a transition; tap the ⧓ to change it: cross-fade, dip to black/white, wipe, slide, zoom, circle or blur, with direction and duration (0.1–3 s).
          • The two clips overlap by the transition's length, so the video gets that much shorter. On upper tracks the clips have to meet exactly; moving one of them removes the transition.
          • The sound is cross-faded too — or switch that off for a hard cut in the middle.
          • Apply to all cuts on this track uses the same transition everywhere; a new transition starts like the last one you chose.
          • The Fade tab fades a clip's picture in from the background or out to it (upper tracks: to transparent). For videos, Picture and sound together keeps the picture and sound fades the same.
          Audio, volume & fades
          • Add → Audio (or drop/paste MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG) puts music or sound at the playhead on an audio track below the main track.
          • Detach audio (clip panel of a video) moves a video's sound onto its own audio track — the video is muted, both can then be cut and moved separately.
          • Audio clips and overlays snap to the start and end of other clips and to the playhead — a blue line shows where (phones vibrate briefly on Android; switchable in Settings). The magnet button below the timeline turns snapping off; hold Alt to move freely for a moment.
          • The Audio tab sets a clip's volume (up to 200 % — a limiter prevents distortion) and fade in / fade out. Fades show as dark ramps on the clip.
          • Track volume and clip volume multiply. 🔊 in the track header mutes a whole track.
          Crop, position & rotate
          • Select a clip and open a tab — the playhead jumps into the clip and you edit directly in the preview.
          • Crop: drag the frame or its corners. Use as canvas makes the crop the video format — with Selected clip only you save just that section.
          • Position: drag the picture (also beyond the edges), use the 3×3 grid or the arrow pad. It snaps to centre and edges — hold Alt to move freely.
          • Size: Fit, Fill or Free — pinch or scroll the mouse wheel over the preview.
          • Each drag or slider move is one undo step.
          Canvas & creating the video
          • Canvas sets the format and what fills empty space: a color or a blurred copy of the main track.
          • Create video… opens the export window: file name, whole timeline or Selected clip only (needs a selected clip), resolution, quality, frame rate, codec.
          • Max. file size may lower the resolution — tick Keep resolution to prevent it. Videos never get a higher bitrate than their source.
          • You can close the window while it's working — the button shows the progress and then Video ready.
          • The preview may stutter with many videos at once — the created video doesn't.
          Saving & other devices
          • Phones keep a working copy of each video in the browser; desktop Chrome/Edge remembers the original file (one click on Allow access after a reload).
          • Missing file? Relink it — renamed files are recognised by their size, all cuts are kept.
          • ⓘ next to the project name shows where each file is kept and how much space it uses.
          • ⇪ Save project file moves a project to another device or makes a backup — with or without the video files.
